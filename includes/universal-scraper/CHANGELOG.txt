INSTALLATION:
-------------
Copy universal-scraper folder to your theme's includes/ directory

Add to functions.php:
require_once get_template_directory() . '/includes/universal-scraper/init.php';

COMPATIBILITY:
--------------

- WooCommerce 3.0+
- PHP 7.0+
