   wp-content/themes/YOUR-NEW-THEME/includes/universal-scraper/

3. Open functions.php in new theme

4. Add at the end of file:

   require_once get_template_directory() . '/includes/universal-scraper/init.php';

5. DONE! Refresh WordPress Admin and see "Content Scraper" menu
